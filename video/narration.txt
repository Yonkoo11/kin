===landing===
When someone dies, one person has to tell twenty or thirty organisations. Five are banks. The rest are the phone carrier, the gym, the storage unit. Each wants something different, and most will not say what until you have waited on hold. Nearly every product in this space assumes you can email a bank about a death. We read their own pages instead. Of seven organisations, exactly one accepts email.
===judge===
This is running now, with one input: the words Wells Fargo. Firecrawl finds their own page, because hard-coded links rot. Of nine we tried by hand, four were wrong and two refused to load. Then the model pulls the requirements out of the page it actually fetched. Nothing on this list is on a timer. Each line strikes through when that step really finishes. The middle line of that address is the one that matters. It carries an internal mail-stop code. No model knows that string, and it appears nowhere in our code. Every contact detail is checked back against the page before it is stored. Anything the model produced that is not literally on that page is dropped, and named. Wells Fargo will not take this by email, so Kin does not pretend otherwise. It produces the postal packet, with that code, and still catches the reply. Where an organisation does accept email, AgentMail runs the exchange end to end. Nothing is ever sent without a person approving it.
